Welcome to the Stardog Open-Source organization. This space hosts community-driven projects that help teams deploy and operate Stardog in modern infrastructure.

## Projects

- [kube-stardog-stack](https://github.com/stardog-oss/kube-stardog-stack): Helm charts for deploying the Stardog ecosystem on Kubernetes.

## Support & Disclaimer

All projects in this organization are released under the Apache 2.0 License and are provided without express warranty or commercial support.

They are supported in the same manner as other community-driven open-source projects through collaborative contributions, issue tracking, and community engagement. As active maintainers and contributors, we will continue to participate in and support these projects within the open-source framework alongside the broader user community.

Important: These projects are provided as open-source artifacts, and users should not establish production runtime dependencies on public artifact repositories. For production environments, organizations are strongly advised to pull, version, and store artifacts in their own internal repositories (for example JFrog, Nexus, OCI registry) to ensure stability, repeatability, and controlled lifecycle management.

This approach ensures a more modern, consolidated, and forward-looking deployment model while aligning with open-source best practices and enterprise governance standards.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from the community. Please open issues and pull requests against the relevant repository. Maintainers may reach out to move your PR to a release branch depending on timing and release planning.
